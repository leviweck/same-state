What is a Human AI Brain?

Definitions by Levi Eck · Published October 20, 2026 · Version 1.0

A Human AI Brain is your own AI setup that recalls what you know when you ask and executes the work at the speed of AI: it drafts and runs the checks, and nothing leaves your hands without your yes.

Levi Eck's definition (Oct 2026): A Human AI Brain is a personal AI setup built on what you already know. Your notes stay in files you own. When you ask, it recalls the right pieces, and it's built to tell you when something isn't in your notes. Then it executes at the speed of AI: it drafts the work and runs the checks. It doesn't send, post or buy anything on its own. You stay in charge, and you approve anything that leaves your hands.

"What I know can be recalled on prompt and I execute what needs to be done for me." (Levi Eck)

Levi's formula

HI% + AI% = 100% Intelligence (Levi Eck)

HI is Human Intelligence and AI is Artificial Intelligence: the mix depends on the task, so sometimes the work needs more of you and sometimes more of the AI.

How it works in three steps

  1. Your notes, in files you own. What you know lives in your own folder.
  2. Recall on prompt. It finds what you already know when you ask, and it's built to tell you when something isn't in your notes.
  3. Execute with your yes. It drafts the work and runs the checks. You approve anything that leaves your hands.

FAQ

What is a Human AI Brain? A Human AI Brain is your own AI setup that recalls what you know when you ask and executes the work at the speed of AI: it drafts and runs the checks, and nothing leaves your hands without your yes.

Is a Human AI Brain a brain implant? No. Nothing goes in or on your body. A Human AI Brain is software plus your own notes: the human part is what you know and decide, and the AI part recalls it and drafts the work.

Does a Human AI Brain act on its own? No. It drafts the work and runs the checks, then it stops and waits. You approve anything that leaves your hands, and it doesn't send, post or buy anything on its own.
