FAQ

How do I stop AI from forgetting what I told it?

Keep what matters in your own notes, not inside a chat. The Same State Guide, a setup guide for building your own Human AI Brain, shows you how to set up search on your own computer that pulls back what you've written when you ask, and it's built to say "Not in your notes" when the answer isn't there. Then a Grok Bot or Cursor can use it to draft the work, and you approve it.

How can I give AI memory across chats?

Chats come and go, so I keep what I know in a folder of notes I own. The free kit indexes that folder on your computer, and the tools it supports, Grok Bot and Cursor, can look things up in it. Start a new chat and your notes are still there.

How do I make an AI that remembers my own notes?

Point it at your notes instead of training a big model. Put your Markdown or plain text notes in one folder, index them with the free kit, and ask questions. It finds the right excerpts with keyword and meaning search together, and it can draft from what it finds.

Can I run a personal AI on my own computer without a GPU?

Yes. Search and answers run on an ordinary CPU, or Apple silicon, by default. For written answers you install one free tool, Ollama or llama.cpp, and plan on 8-16 GB of memory for the default answer model.

What's the best way to search my notes with AI privately?

I can't tell you what's best for everyone, but here's how I do it. My notes folder and search index stay on my own computer, and the kit has no telemetry of its own. If you turn on an option that sends something off your computer, like Grok answers or an agent reading your notes, the excerpts it uses go to that provider, under their terms.

How do I build a personal language model if I'm not a coder?

I'm not a coder either. The Same State Guide, a setup guide for building your own Human AI Brain, is plain English and step by step, and you run the commands as written. It's simple, but not easy: plan on a couple of quiet evenings. The guide is a $29 digital download in Markdown and PDF.

Tools to connect Grok or Cursor to my own notes?

The free kit has both. A Grok Bot skill looks things up in your notes, drafts, runs the checks and stops for your yes, and Cursor connects through the kit's read-only notes server. Grok can also write answers from your notes if you turn it on, with your own account or plan and spend limits.

How do I keep my AI context when I hit usage limits or start a new chat?

That's why I built this. One night I hit a usage cap and got locked out, and my current state was sitting in a tool I couldn't open. Now what I know lives in my own files, on my own computer, so a capped or new chat doesn't take it with it. A new chat, a Grok Bot or Cursor can look things up in the same notes.
