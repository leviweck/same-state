Same State Guide: build your own Human AI Brain

A plain-English, step-by-step guide to a personal language model you run yourself, one that remembers what you know and drafts what needs doing.

What if you always remembered? What if you always followed through? What if you had the education and means to deliver?

That's what I'm building toward with a Human AI Brain: "what I know can be recalled on prompt and I execute what needs to be done for me."

Here's what follow-through means. It pulls back what you've already written when you ask, and it's built to flag when an answer isn't in your notes. Test it on yours. It drafts for you. The kit can't send anything. Drafts go through a safety check and wait for your approval.

Written for people who aren't engineers. Simple, but not easy.

What you need: your own notes and a computer that can run the kit. No GPU needed. Search and answers run on an ordinary CPU, with a free tool you install yourself.

What's included:

  • Module 1: what a personal language model is, in plain English
  • Module 2: setting up where it runs, your models and your notes, plus how to work out your own costs
  • Module 3: how it recalls what you know, using keyword and meaning search together, and how to test it on a sample set built from fake notes
  • Module 4: the safety layer, where every draft gets checked and the kit can't send anything
  • Module 5: putting it to work with Grok, Grok Bot and Cursor: optional Grok answers through the xAI API with spend limits, a Grok Bot skill that looks things up in your notes, drafts, runs the checks and stops for your yes, and changing the kit in Cursor with the safety gates on (Grok and Grok Bot need your own xAI account or plan)
